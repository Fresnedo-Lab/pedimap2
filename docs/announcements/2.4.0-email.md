Subject: Pedimap 2.4.0 is ready – FlexQTL and Binko files, IBD bars on the chart

Hi all,

A new version of Pedimap 2 (2.4.0) is out. It brings together two releases
of work: reading FlexQTL and Binko.jl output correctly, and drawing IBD
haplotypes on the chart. If you have 2.1.2 or later, Pedimap will offer the
update the next time you open it; you can also click ⓘ About → Check for
updates at the top right of the Pedimap window. If you have an older
version, please install it yourself:

1. Go to https://github.com/Fresnedo-Lab/pedimap2/releases/latest
2. Use the "Which file do I need?" table at the top of that page to pick
   the file for your computer, then download and install it.

**If you opened a FlexQTL file with more than one linkage group in 2.2.2 or
earlier, please open the original file again.** Those versions mixed up the
IBD probabilities of such files: where two linkage groups share a position
(such as 0 cM), only the last group's values were kept. `.dat` and JSON
files saved from such a file by those versions carry the mixed-up data, so
start again from the file FlexQTL wrote.

**IBD bars on the chart.** Open the `pedimap.ped` file that FlexQTL or
Binko.jl writes with **📂 Open File**, then pick a linkage group under
**IBD bars** in the toolbar. Every plant's box then shows two bars, one per
chromosome copy (from the first parent on the left, from the second on the
right), running along the linkage group's map. Each bar is colored by the
founder alleles it most likely comes from: each founder has its own color,
in a light and a dark shade for its two alleles, and very small
probabilities are drawn together in dark gray. Hold the mouse over a bar to
see the founder allele, its founder and the probability at that spot.

- In **⚙**, under **IBD bars**, you can make the bars wider or narrower and
  choose the palette. "By founder allele" gives every founder allele its
  own color, which tells colors apart better when there are many founders.
  Pedimap remembers your choice.
- **Export image** (PNG, SVG or PDF) draws the bars as on screen, with a
  legend that lists each founder and the numbers of its alleles in their
  colors.

Also new since 2.2.2:

- **Marker genotypes:** click a plant to see, under **Markers**, its two
  alleles at every locus, grouped by linkage group, with the genotype code as
  a small colored badge. In FlexQTL and Binko files, hold the mouse over a
  badge to see what the code means (observed, filled in, judged an error, …).
- **IBD probabilities in the details panel:** under **IBD**, pick a linkage
  group and a position to see which founder allele each of the plant's two
  chromosome copies most likely comes from, with the founder's name and the
  probability.
- **Color by a marker:** **Color by** also offers **Genotype code at
  marker…** and **Allele at marker…** (how many copies of an allele each
  plant carries), with a legend on the chart and in exported images.
- **Marker data**, next to the population name, summarizes the file's loci,
  linkage groups, IBD positions and the genotype codes it uses.
- **Searching works as it should:** typing in the search box no longer zooms
  or moves the chart, so names with _ or - (such as G1_001 or 81015-045) can
  be searched. The chart's own keys (arrows to move, + and - to zoom) work
  after you click the chart.
- Parents are labeled as your file names them: "Parent 1" and "Parent 2" for
  FlexQTL and Binko files, "Female parent" and "Male parent" for files that
  say FEMALE and MALE.
- Names on colored plants are always readable: black on light colors, white
  on dark ones, on screen and in exported images.
- Mistakes in a data file are reported with their line number, and problems
  that do not stop a file from loading are shown above the chart in plain
  words.
- In exported images, a legend sits below a top-to-bottom chart, and the
  page grows so the legend never covers the chart.

If something looks wrong, click ⓘ About, note the version number, and send
it to me with the name of the file.
